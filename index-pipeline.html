01 · The Pipeline

Raw data flows through four stages — never more.

Every project I touch follows the same pipeline. It keeps the work honest: collect only what matters, clean what's broken, prove the insight, then act on it.

PIPELINE.DAG · RUNNING LIVE
STAGE 01
Collect
ODK Collect forms, APIs, surveys — offline-first, structured.
STAGE 02
Clean
Python + Pandas validation. Missing, malformed, outlier — fixed.
STAGE 03
Analyse
SQL, Dash, Plotly. The insight survives a room of sceptics.
STAGE 04
Decide
MIS reports on decision-makers' desks — Monday morning.
02 · About

One person, two disciplines.

Data analyst by training, developer by practice — and the rare mix of both on the ground in public health.

I'm a public health data professional and full-stack developer working as a Gandhi Fellow with Piramal Foundation in Muzaffarpur, Bihar. My day starts in the field — ASHA workers, ANMs, PHC staff, district officials — and ends with Python scripts, SQL queries, and pipelines that turn raw field data into decisions.

I work the full data lifecycle: ODK Collect & ODK Central forms in the field, cleaning & validation in Python, and MIS reports that land on district officials' desks. Outside health data, I've shipped production MERN apps with JWT auth, REST APIs, Nginx and PM2.
