Current Question

How does Matru Suraksha handle zero connectivity in remote Bihar sub-centres and escalate high-risk cases?

verified Grounded (98.4%)

1. Offline-First Architecture (Dexie.js & IndexedDB)

Matru Suraksha was engineered for zero-bandwidth rural health sub-centres across Bihar. The front-end leverages Dexie.js over IndexedDB to maintain an immutable local store. Auxiliary Nurse Midwives (ANMs) register antenatal checkups, track 8 critical maternal danger signs (severe anemia, pre-eclampsia, systolic BP > 140, gestational diabetes), and generate patient records entirely without internet.

2. Optimistic Sync & Cryptographic Conflict Resolution

When connectivity (intermittent 2G/3G) is detected, sync queues push updates to Fastify backend services. Records use SHA-256 state hashing for optimistic concurrency control, ensuring zero data loss even when ANMs and Community Health Officers (CHOs) update vitals simultaneously.

3. Deterministic 15-Day High-Risk Pregnancy (HRP) Escalation

Any pregnancy flagged with high-risk parameters starts an automated 15-day clinical clock:

  • Day 1: SMS alerts sent automatically to designated ANM and CHO.
  • Day 12 (if unverified): Escalated to the Block Health Manager (BHM) dashboard with priority overdue flag.
  • Day 15 (SLA breach): Automatically reported in District Health Society (DHS) escalation report for maternal mortality prevention.
